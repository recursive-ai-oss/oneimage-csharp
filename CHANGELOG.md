# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `AlphaComposition.Plus` (descriptor name `plus`): the Porter-Duff plus operator, CSS `plus-lighter`. Premultiplied color and alpha of overlay and backdrop add and clamp at 1; an empty overlay leaves the backdrop, and an opaque backdrop stays opaque.
- `OneImage.Text`: right-to-left text. The Unicode Bidirectional Algorithm (UAX #9, Unicode 17) runs per paragraph, lines are reordered after wrapping, and `TextOptions.TextDirection` (`Auto`, `LeftToRight`, `RightToLeft`) sets the base direction. `TextAlignment.Start` and `End` follow the paragraph direction. Left-to-right text without right-to-left characters skips the algorithm.
- `OneImage.Text`: an Arabic joining shaper (also used for Syriac and N'Ko) with per-glyph form features, and Hebrew with points and cantillation marks.
- `OneImage.Text`: complex-script shaping. An Indic shaper (Devanagari, Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam: syllables, reph, half forms, pre-base matras, split vowels, dotted circle for broken clusters), a Universal Shaping Engine shaper (Khmer, Myanmar, Sinhala, Tibetan, Balinese, Javanese and the other USE scripts) and Thai/Lao shaping (SARA AM). Unicode property tables for them are generated by `tools/gen_unicode_tables.py`. Syllable length, reordering and run size are bounded. Line breaking for Thai, Lao, Khmer and Myanmar stays UAX #14 without dictionaries. Against SixLabors.Fonts 2.1.3 on 1,042 cases in 17 Noto subsets, 897 match exactly, 119 differ deliberately (listed with reasons in `docs/text.md`) and the reference throws on 26. A Devanagari paragraph measures in 1.4 ms against 4.0 ms and draws in 2.5 ms against 10.2 ms; a Khmer paragraph 0.53 against 2.8 ms and 1.4 against 7.0 ms (local smoke, loaded host). A `complex` fuzz target covers the shapers.
- `OneImage.Text`: vertical and bottom-to-top layout (`TextOptions.LayoutMode`, with the members of SixLabors.Fonts' enum), from `vhea`, `vmtx`, `VORG`, the `vert`/`vrt2` features and the UAX #50 orientation property; TrueType hinting (`TextOptions.HintingMode.Standard` runs the font's instructions at the drawing size, y positions only, under hard limits; measuring is unchanged); and COLR version 1 paint graphs (gradients, transforms, clips, composite modes) painted by `DrawText`. Compared with SixLabors.Fonts 2.1.3 (vertical: 296 of 296 measurement cases; hinting: mean 0.45 against 1.47 levels unhinted at 9-16 px) and SkiaSharp (COLR v1: mean 0.36 levels). `fuzz/run.sh font` covers hinting and paint graphs. See `docs/text.md`.
- Tests run the official `BidiTest.txt` and `BidiCharacterTest.txt` in full and compare glyphs and layout with SixLabors.Fonts 2.1.3; a `bidi` fuzz target covers mixed-direction text.
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg` (not yet published): SVG as an image format. Draws shapes, paths, solid fills and strokes (caps, joins, dashes), opacity layers, transforms, `viewBox`, nested `svg`, `use` and `symbol` at any size, one region at a time, with limits on instantiation. Markers, filters, text, images and style sheets are not drawn yet.
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg`: paint servers. `linearGradient` and `radialGradient` (both unit systems, `gradientTransform`, `spreadMethod`, stop colors and opacities, `href` inheritance with loops handled, SVG 2 focal point and `fr`, the degenerate cases the specification defines), `pattern` (tile units, content units, `viewBox`, `patternTransform`, nesting, `href` inheritance; the tile is drawn at the resolution it is seen at and capped by the new `SvgLimits.MaxPatternTilePixels`), `url(#id) fallback` paints, `paint-order` and `context-fill`/`context-stroke` through `use`.
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg`: filters, part one. The `filter` property with `url()` references and CSS filter functions; `feGaussianBlur`, `feOffset`, `feFlood`, `feMerge`, `feBlend`, `feComposite`, `feColorMatrix`, `feConvolveMatrix`, `feImage` and `feDropShadow` evaluated in linear-light float with per-primitive color spaces, filter regions and subregions, on rotated and skewed content, tile-independent; new `SvgLimits.MaxFilterPixels`, `MaxFilterPrimitives` and `MaxFilterDepth`.
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg`: text. `text`, `tspan`, `a`, `tref` and `textPath` with per-character `x`/`y`/`dx`/`dy`/`rotate`, `text-anchor`, baselines and baseline shifts, `textLength`, letter and word spacing, decorations, `xml:space`, bidirectional text, small caps, font fallback and paint servers on text, and text as a clip path. Fonts come from the new `SvgFontOptions` (files, bytes, streams, generic families, fallback families, installed fonts) passed to `SvgFormat`; `SvgFontOptions.DefaultFontSize` (default 16, the CSS `medium` size) sets the initial font size and the base of the named sizes; `SvgLimits.MaxTextCharacters` bounds the text a document may hold. `writing-mode` vertical text (ideographs upright with the font's vertical forms, other characters rotated, `dx`/`dy`/`text-anchor` along the column), text style that follows the `use` element's ancestors, `opacity` on a `tspan` applied per span, and ligatures split where a character has its own `dx`, `dy` or a different `rotate`. 279 of the 383 text tests of resvg's suite pass (72.8%; variable fonts and color fonts are not supported yet).
- `OneImage.Text`: `TextShaping` (`GetBidiLevels`, `GetVisualOrder`, `ShapeRun`, `IsUprightInVerticalText`), `ShapedCluster` (advance, outline, `VerticalAdvance`, `GetUprightOutline`), `ShapingOptions` (including `Vertical`) and `FontFaceInfo`, for callers that lay text out themselves: shaped clusters with their advances and outlines, one bidirectional paragraph at a time, upright or rotated in a vertical column.
- `OneImage.Text`: subtables the shaping engine cannot use now count against a run's work budget, so a font with lookups of tens of thousands of them no longer costs seconds per run (a fuzz-found font that took 3.5 s per 1,200 glyphs now takes milliseconds; shaping results are unchanged).
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg`: filters, part two. `feComponentTransfer`, `feMorphology` (cost independent of the radius), `feTile`, `feTurbulence` (fractal noise and turbulence, seeds, octaves, stitching), `feDisplacementMap`, `feDiffuseLighting` and `feSpecularLighting` with distant, point and spot lights, a zero-sum `feConvolveMatrix` kernel without a divisor, and the default subregion of tiled inputs. Filters with a wide margin (a large blur) compute their output in shared blocks instead of refiltering the margin for every tile: a 4096-pixel render under a blur of 30 units went from 9.8 s to 0.85 s. Reference-suite `filters`: 388 of 406 files (was 291).
- `Brush.LinearGradient(..., Matrix3x2 transform, ...)` places a gradient through an affine transform; `Brush.RadialGradient(center, radius, focalCenter, focalRadius, ...)` is a two-circle (focal) gradient with an optional transform; `Brush.FromImage(source, ImageSampling.Bilinear, ...)` reads an image brush with bilinear sampling on premultiplied color (new `ImageSampling` enum). The existing brushes are unchanged.
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg`: style sheets (`<style>` with type, class, id, attribute, combinator and structural selectors, specificity, source order, `!important`, `inherit`, `initial`, `unset`), `switch` with `systemLanguage` (`new SvgFormat(languages: ...)`, default `en`), `requiredFeatures` and `requiredExtensions`, `image` with `data:` URIs (any raster format the library reads, and nested SVG drawn as vectors, with `preserveAspectRatio` and `image-rendering`), and `transform-origin`. New `SvgLimits`: `MaxCssBytes`, `MaxCssRules`, `MaxCssMatchSteps`, `MaxImageBytes`, `MaxTotalImageBytes`, `MaxImageDepth`. Nothing external is ever fetched.
- `DecoderCapabilities.RenderAtSize` and `DecodeRequest.TargetWidth`/`TargetHeight`: a vector decoder is asked for the frame at its final size when a resize sits directly on the load, so nothing is scaled as a raster.

- `Image.ApplyMask(mask, channel, x, y, linear)` and `MaskChannel`: multiplies an image's coverage by a per-pixel mask taken from another image (its alpha, or its luminance times alpha), lazily and tile by tile; everything outside the mask becomes transparent. This is the layer-mask operation the SVG renderer composites masked groups with.
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg`: `clipPath` (units, transform, per-child `clip-rule`, nested clips on the clip and on its children, `use` children, cycles broken) and `mask` (units, regions, `mask-type`, masks on masks, cycles broken) on shapes, groups, `use`, nested `svg` and the root, plus `overflow` on nested viewports. Clips are resolved with the polygon boolean engine, so clipped regions draw only the nodes they touch; each clip child counts as 16 instances against the instantiation limit.
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg`: markers (`marker-start`/`mid`/`end` and the `marker` shorthand on paths, lines, polylines, polygons and basic shapes; `markerUnits`, `refX`/`refY`, `viewBox`, `overflow`, `orient` as `auto`, `auto-start-reverse` or an angle; context paints; `paint-order` placement; nested and recursive markers under the instance budget), `mix-blend-mode` with every CSS blend mode, and `isolation`.
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg`: `transform-origin` also applies to `gradientTransform`, `patternTransform` and the `clipPath` transform; `mix-blend-mode: plus-lighter` is the Porter-Duff plus operator.

### Changed

- `Resize` with `Fit.Cover` pushes its crop down to a decoder that can produce a region.
- Drawing and SVG rendering cost less per shape: a `Canvas` fill reuses its flattened path, edge table and buckets from one fill to the next on a thread, the tile rasterizer sums and clears only the accumulator cells an edge touched, tile workers no longer share one scratch bag, and 8-bit/float RGBA row conversion skips its temporary float rows. Output is unchanged. In `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Svg`, opacity and blend layers are read in place and give their tiles back to the pool, which cuts the allocation of a blend-heavy drawing from 65 MB to 7 MB per render; an icon at 256 px renders in 118 µs (298 µs before) and the 3,000-shape illustration in 11.5 ms (25.2 ms).

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Text`, a separate package for fonts and text: `FontCollection` loads TrueType, OpenType/CFF, TrueType Collections and WOFF 1.0 (own inflate); `TextOptions` (origin, wrapping, line spacing, tab width, dpi, horizontal, vertical and text alignment, kerning), `TextMeasurer` (advance, ink bounds, lines, character boxes), `TextBuilder.GeneratePath` (text as a `Path`) and `DrawText` on `Image` and `Canvas` with a brush and/or pen. Outlines, advances, kerning, line counts and bounds match SixLabors.Fonts 2.1.3; rendering agrees with ImageSharp.Drawing 2.1.7 DrawText to coverage differences. See `docs/text.md`. A fuzz target `font` covers the parsers.
- OpenType shaping in `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Text`: GDEF glyph classes and mark filtering sets, GSUB lookup types 1-8 and GPOS types 1-9 with all lookup flags, so ligatures (`fi`, `ffl`), `ccmp` compositions, language forms (`locl`), contextual alternates, pair kerning and mark positioning on bases, ligatures and other marks work for Latin, Greek and Cyrillic. `TextOptions.Features` (`FontFeature.On("smcp")`, `Off("liga")`, alternates by value) and `TextOptions.Language` choose features and language forms; `KerningMode.None` is the `kern` feature off. Lookups are bounded in depth, work and growth, and damaged layout tables are ignored. Glyph ids and origins match SixLabors.Fonts 2.1.3 on Noto Sans; measuring and drawing a ligature-heavy paragraph is 8x and 19x faster. See `docs/text.md`.
- Unicode text segmentation in `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Text`: wrapping now ends lines at the break opportunities of the Unicode line breaking algorithm (UAX #14, Unicode 17.0: spaces, hyphens, soft hyphen, zero width space, no-break space and word joiner, quotation marks and brackets, numbers, East Asian text, Korean syllables, emoji sequences, flags), and never inside an extended grapheme cluster (UAX #29): `GetCharacterBounds` returns one box per cluster. New `TextOptions.WordBreaking` (`Standard`, `BreakAll`, `KeepAll`, `BreakWord`) and `TextOptions.TextJustification` (`None`, `InterWord`, `InterCharacter`), with ImageSharp's names and behavior. The property tables (72 KB) are generated from the Unicode Character Database by `tools/gen_unicode_tables.py`; `LineBreakTest.txt` (19,338 cases) and `GraphemeBreakTest.txt` (766 cases) pass in full. Ordinary text wraps at the same places as SixLabors.Fonts 2.1.3 (1,590 cases, none different), and justification matches it to 0.0001 px; text with break opportunities inside words differs, and `docs/text.md` lists why.
- Rich text, font fallback, text on a path and installed fonts in `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Text`. `TextOptions.TextRuns` (`TextRun`: its own font and size, `TextAttributes` super- and subscript, `TextDecorations` underline, strikeout and overline at the font's positions and thicknesses, brush, pen and per-decoration pens) works through layout, measuring, `TextBuilder.GeneratePath` and `DrawText`; lines mix sizes and faces. `TextOptions.FallbackFontFamilies` shapes characters a font lacks in the first fallback family that has them. `TextOptions.Path` lays text along a `Path`, rotating each glyph to the tangent, with open paths continued straight and closed ones wrapped. `SystemFonts` / `SystemFontSet` find installed fonts by scanning the standard directories of Windows, macOS and Linux, reading only name tables until a family is requested. Glyph ink boxes, advances, line counts and decoration segments match SixLabors.Fonts 2.1.3 to 0.01 px or better and text on a path matches ImageSharp.Drawing 2.1.7 to 0.001 px on lines; the differences (wrapping with fallback, nested runs, one reference kerning quirk, closed-path edges) are listed in `docs/text.md`. Run counts (16,384), fallback families (32), scan depth and file counts are bounded. Color glyphs (COLR v0) work inside runs and in fallback faces: a run's brush fills the foreground layers, layers with their own color keep it, and the pens draw last. New fuzz targets `richtext` and `sysfont`.

### Changed

- Strokes whose outline lies on itself along an edge (a path that doubles back, a closed two-point figure, a cusp) are drawn with the coverage of the union, not the sum: their edge pixels were up to twice as dense. The outline is merged only when such a turn (sharper than about 172 degrees) occurs, so other strokes are unchanged in output and time (stroke benchmark rows within noise).
- `Canvas.Fill(..., linear: true)` (and `Draw` with `linear: true`) no longer reads the canvas through a public snapshot: without an outside snapshot it swaps in fresh pooled tiles and returns the replaced ones, and it gives back the tiles its one-shot graph cached, which used to fill the shared tile cache (up to its 256 MiB budget) until the canvas was collected. A linear-light fill of a circle on a 2000 x 2000 canvas allocates 65 MB instead of 82 MB, with identical output. The color for a linear-light solid fill is now computed without leaving a pooled tile behind.
- Linear-light fills and strokes (`linear: true`) on 8-bit gray and RGB images and canvases without an embedded profile now decode, blend and encode only the covered pixels instead of converting whole tiles to float. On a 2000 x 2000 Rgba32 image a solid circle fill took 74 ms and allocated 52 MB, and on a canvas 242 ms and 65 MB; now both take about 2 ms (encoded: 1.9 and 1.3 ms) and allocate 24 KB. Gradient, Multiply and stroke fills are within 1.5x of their encoded cost. Output agrees with the whole-tile conversion path to 1 level at 8 bits (untouched pixels are now never rewritten). Other formats and images with an embedded profile keep the whole-tile path.
- `Canvas.Clip` no longer copies tiles through a public snapshot: it swaps in fresh pooled tiles and returns the replaced and cached ones to the pool. A blur clipped to a circle on a 2000 x 2000 canvas allocates 88 KB instead of 9.6 MB, at the same speed and with identical output.

- WOFF 2.0 fonts in `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Text`: `FontCollection.Add` and `AddCollection` read single fonts and collections, with the transformed `glyf`/`loca` and `hmtx` tables, on the package's own Brotli decoder (RFC 7932, safe managed code; the BCL decoder is native and is not used). Declared sizes and expansion are bounded before anything is allocated. Faces, outlines, advances, kerning and shaping equal the source fonts'. A fuzz target `brotli` joins `font`. See `docs/text.md`.
- Color glyphs in `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Text`: `TextOptions.ColorFontSupport` (`MicrosoftColrFormat` by default, as in SixLabors.Fonts; `None` turns it off) and `ColorPaletteIndex` make `DrawText` on `Image` and `Canvas` paint COLR version 0 layers with CPAL palette colors or the brush (palette index 0xFFFF); `Font.ColorPaletteCount` and `Font.HasColorGlyph`. Matches ImageSharp.Drawing 2.1.7 to a mean of 0.07 levels on a layered test font. COLR version 1 glyphs draw as their plain outline. Text as a path stays monochrome.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- Vector drawing foundation (`OneImage.Drawing`): `Path`, `PathBuilder` (lines, quadratic and cubic Béziers, SVG elliptical arcs), shape factories (rectangle, rounded rectangle, ellipse, circle, polygon, regular polygon, star, combine), transforms, reverse, length, bounds, flattening, and strict bounded SVG path-data parsing (`Path.TryParseSvg`). `Image.Fill`, `Image.Clear`, `Canvas.Fill` and `Canvas.Clear` fill paths with a solid `Brush`: exact-area anti-aliasing (or none), non-zero and even-odd rules, blend modes, Porter-Duff operators, opacity, a transform, and linear-light blending. `Image` fills are lazy and a run of fills computes in one pass; `Canvas` fills draw in place in parallel with identical bytes. See `docs/drawing.md`. A fuzz target `svg` covers the path parser.
- Strokes: `Image.Draw` / `Canvas.Draw` with a `Pen` (any brush, width, `StrokeJoin` Miter/Round/Bevel/Square with a miter limit, `StrokeCap` Butt/Round/Square, dash patterns with offset, presets Dash/Dot/DashDot/DashDotDot) or a color and width; helpers `DrawLine`, `DrawPolyline`, `DrawPolygon`, `DrawBeziers`, `DrawRectangle`, `DrawEllipse`; and `Path.GenerateOutline` returning the stroke as a `Path`. Overlapping parts of one stroke are painted once, so translucent pens are even. 6 to 7 times faster than ImageSharp.Drawing on strokes with a fifth to a quarter of the allocation; see `docs/drawing.md`.
- Brushes for fills (`OneImage.Drawing.Brush`): linear, radial and elliptic gradients with color stops and the repetition modes none, repeat, reflect and dont-fill; a path gradient; pattern brushes with the hatch presets (horizontal, vertical, forward and backward diagonal, 10%, 20%, min); an image brush with region, offset, transform and tiling; and a recolor brush. Gradients interpolate premultiplied colors in encoded values by default and blend in linear light with `linear: true`, which now works with every brush. `Brush` is public to derive from: implement `FillRow` (and `ReadsBackdrop` for brushes that read the existing pixels). Opaque brush colors over fully covered pixels are written directly, through a vector kernel for 8-bit RGBA. Agrees with ImageSharp.Drawing 2.1.7 to rounding on every brush and mode; see `docs/drawing.md` for the differences.
- `Image.Composite(..., blend:, alpha:)`: all 24 Photoshop/W3C blend modes (`BlendMode`: Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Darken, Lighten, Color Dodge/Burn, Hard/Soft/Vivid/Linear/Pin Light, Hard Mix, Difference, Exclusion, Add, Subtract, Linear Burn, Divide, Hue, Saturation, Color, Luminosity) and the twelve Porter-Duff operators (`AlphaComposition`). Normal source-over output is unchanged.
- `Image.Perspective`: a projective (perspective) transform from a `ProjectiveMatrix`, a `ProjectiveTransformBuilder` (taper, rotation, scale, skew, translation, matrices, with `Append` and `Prepend` forms) or four corner points. `ProjectiveMatrix` also builds quad-to-quad maps.
- `Fit.BoxPad` and `Fit.Min` (ImageSharp's `ResizeMode.BoxPad` and `Min`), the aliases `Fit.Max` and `Fit.Crop`, and `Image.ResizeInto` for resizing into a rectangle of a canvas (`ResizeMode.Manual`).
- Resampling filters `Hermite`, `Lanczos5`, `Lanczos8`, `Robidoux`, `RobidouxSharp`, `Spline` and `Welch`, with the aliases `Bicubic` and `Triangle`, in `Resize` and in the rotate, affine and perspective samplers.
- Neighbourhood effects, matching ImageSharp 3.1.12: `Pixelate`, `OilPaint`, `Vignette`, `Glow`, `BokehBlur`, `GaussianSharpen`, `DetectEdges` (`EdgeDetector`: Sobel, Prewitt, Scharr, RobertsCross, Kayyali, Laplacian3x3/5x5, LaplacianOfGaussian, Kirsch, Robinson) and `EntropyCrop`. `OilPaint` centers its window, where ImageSharp's is shifted; see the migration guide.
- Color matrix: `Image.ApplyColorMatrix(ColorMatrix)` with a public `ColorMatrix` (ImageSharp's 5x4 layout), and the looks built on it: `Sepia`, `BlackWhite`, `Kodachrome`, `Lomograph`, `Polaroid`, `ColorBlindness(ColorBlindnessMode)` and `Opacity`. `Lomograph` and `Polaroid` include ImageSharp's vignette and glow; `ColorMatrix.Lomograph` and `.Polaroid` are the plain matrices. They fuse with the other color ops into one pass per tile, and a color chain directly on a decode now runs on rows as they are decoded when the whole frame is read at once.
- `Image.HistogramEqualization`: global, adaptive tile interpolation and adaptive sliding window equalization with histogram clipping, luminance levels and tile count, matching ImageSharp 3.1.12 within 1 level at 8 bits (`HistogramEqualizationMethod`). The luminance is mapped and written to all color channels, as in ImageSharp.
- `RecursiveAI.OneImage.Web`, a separate package with ASP.NET Core middleware for on-the-fly image processing (`AddOneImage()` / `UseOneImage()`): URL commands for size, fit, anchor and focal point, filter, background, format, quality and orientation, with ImageSharp.Web query names accepted too; presets and a presets-only mode; web root, folder, `IFileProvider` and custom providers; a disk cache with configurable layout, ETag and Last-Modified validators with 304 answers, source-change invalidation and single processing of concurrent identical requests; HMAC-signed URLs with a signer and a Razor tag helper; request hooks; and size, source and concurrency limits. See `docs/web.md`.
- Histograms, thresholds and dithering, matching ImageSharp 3.1.12: `Image.AutoLevel`, `Image.AdaptiveThreshold`, and a public `Image.Dither(palette, dither, scale, fastMatch)` (`fastMatch` defaults to true: ImageSharp's order-dependent cell matching, bit-identical to its output; `false` matches each pixel exactly) and `BinaryDither`. The `Dither` enum gains Atkinson, Burkes, JarvisJudiceNinke, Sierra2, Sierra3, SierraLite, StevensonArce, Stucki, Bayer2x2, Ordered3x3, Bayer4x4, Bayer8x8 and Bayer16x16 (they also work in `Quantize` and the GIF encoder). `Quantizer` (Wu, Octree, WebSafe, Werner) selects the palette builder in `Quantize` and `GifEncoderOptions.Quantizer`, and `KnownPalettes.WebSafe` and `KnownPalettes.Werner` are public. Existing GIF and indexed-PNG output is unchanged.
- Boolean operations on paths: `PathBoolean` (`path.Boolean(BooleanOperation, other, ...)`, `Union`, `Intersect`, `Subtract`, `Xor`, `UnionAll`, `Resolve`, `Offset`) with exact integer geometry on a 1/256 px grid, per-operand non-zero or even-odd rules, and polygon results without overlaps. `Image.Clip(path, operations)` / `Canvas.Clip` run any operations only inside a path with anti-aliased edges, and `Fill` / `Draw` take `clip:` to limit a shape to a clip path. A 200-circle union takes 7 ms against ImageSharp.Drawing's 11 ms with one seventh of the allocation, and a Gaussian blur clipped to a circle on 2000 x 2000 takes 12-13 ms against 56 ms; see `docs/drawing.md`.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-22

First release, published on NuGet as `RecursiveAI.OneImage` (namespace `OneImage`).

### Added

- Lazy, immutable `Image` and mutable tiled `Canvas` on one engine, with O(1) snapshots.
- Codecs: PNG (including APNG and indexed output), JPEG (baseline, progressive, arithmetic, 12-bit, CMYK, shrink-on-load), WebP (lossy, lossless, animation), GIF (animation, quantizing, dithering), TIFF (including BigTIFF, CCITT and multi-page), BMP and ICO.
- ICC color management (v2 and v4 profiles, built-in profiles, a generic CMYK profile), resampling in linear light, geometry, tone and filter operations, analysis, and normal-mode compositing.
- `Image.Thumbnail`, which applies EXIF orientation, converts to sRGB and strips EXIF, XMP and IPTC by default.
- Size limits on by default, checked from headers before allocation; typed exceptions; a fuzz target for every decoder and parser.
- Depends only on the .NET base class library; NativeAOT- and trim-compatible.
- Documentation: a guide, a migration guide from ImageSharp and System.Drawing, a comparison with other libraries, and a [performance page](https://github.com/recursive-ai-oss/oneimage-csharp/blob/v1.0.0/docs/performance.md) with the benchmark method and every loss.
