after this library; it is not a Fogra profile and Fogra has not certified, approved or endorsed it.

## Noto font subsets (test data only)

The files in `tests/testdata/fonts/` are subsets of Noto Sans (Copyright 2015 Google LLC) and Noto Sans CJK (Copyright 2014-2021
Adobe), made by `tools/TextFonts/make-test-fonts.py`: fewer glyphs, hinting removed, and in one face a synthetic legacy `kern`
table. The fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the license text is `tests/testdata/fonts/OFL.txt`. They are
used only by the test and benchmark projects and are not part of the shipped package.

The `.woff2` files next to them are the same subsets converted with the reference WOFF2 compressor (`tools/TextFonts/make-woff2-fonts.py`; one also carries the optional `hmtx` transform), under the same license. `OneImageColorTest-V0.ttf` and `-V1.ttf` are fonts of geometric shapes drawn by `tools/TextFonts/make-color-fonts.py` (COLR and CPAL tables); they contain no third-party glyphs and are covered by this project's licenses.

`tests/testdata/fonts/NotoSans-Shaping-Subset.ttf` is a further Noto Sans subset (Copyright 2015 Google LLC, SIL Open Font License 1.1, same license text) made by `tools/TextFonts/make-shaping-fonts.py`: Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and the combining marks, hinting removed, with all of the font's GSUB, GPOS and GDEF layout kept. It is used only by the test and benchmark projects.

`tests/testdata/fonts/NotoSansArabic-Subset-Regular.ttf` (Noto Sans Arabic, Copyright 2015-2020 Google LLC) and `NotoSansHebrew-Subset-Regular.ttf` (Noto Sans Hebrew, Copyright 2019 Google Inc.) are subsets made with fontTools' `pyftsubset` (the needed letters, marks and digits, all layout features kept, hinting removed). `NotoSans-Bidi-Subset.ttf` merges those two and the Noto Sans subset into one font. All three are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (same license text) and used only by the test, benchmark and fuzz projects.

`tests/testdata/fonts/Noto{Sans,Serif}<Script>-Subset-Regular.ttf` for Balinese, Bengali, Devanagari, Gujarati, Gurmukhi, Javanese, Kannada, Khmer, Lao, Malayalam, Myanmar, Oriya, Sinhala, Tamil, Telugu, Thai (Noto Sans) and Tibetan (Noto Serif Tibetan) are subsets of the Noto script fonts (Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors, https://github.com/notofonts/<script>; Bengali Copyright 2025), made by `tools/TextFonts/make-complex-fonts.py` with the needed letters, signs and marks, all layout tables kept and hinting removed. They are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (same license text) and used only by the test, benchmark and fuzz projects.

`tests/testdata/fonts/NotoSans-Hinted-Subset.ttf` is a Latin subset of Noto Sans (Copyright 2015 Google LLC, SIL Open Font License 1.1, same license text) made by `tools/TextFonts/make-hinted-fonts.py` with its TrueType instructions (`fpgm`, `prep`, `cvt `, glyph programs) kept, for the hinting tests. Test and benchmark projects only.

`tests/testdata/fonts/ColrV1-TestGlyphs.ttf` is the static COLR version 1 test font of the googlefonts/color-fonts repository (https://github.com/googlefonts/color-fonts, `fonts/test_glyphs-glyf_colr_1.ttf`, built 2024-05-27), used unmodified under the Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) by the test project only.

## Brotli (static dictionary and test data)

`src/OneImage.Text/Compression/brotli-dictionary.bin` is the static dictionary of RFC 7932 (Appendix A, 122,784 bytes), and `BrotliTables.cs` holds the word transforms of its Appendix B and the context lookup tables of its section 7.1, all extracted from the RFC text by `tools/BrotliTables/gen.py`. The RFC is published by the IETF Trust under its license for code components; the dictionary is also distributed by the Brotli project under the MIT license below. The decoder itself is written from the RFC.

The files in `tests/testdata/brotli/` are test vectors from the Brotli project's `tests/testdata` (Copyright (c) 2009, 2010, 2013-2016 by the Brotli Authors), used under the MIT license whose text is in `tests/testdata/brotli/LICENSE`. They are used only by the test project.
