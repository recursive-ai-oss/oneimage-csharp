## FOGRA39 characterization data

`data/FOGRA39.txt` is the unmodified FOGRA39 characterization data, copied from the ICC characterization data registry
(https://registry.color.org/cmyk-registry/chardata/FOGRA39.txt). Originator: Fogra, www.fogra.org.

Fogra's terms of use permit free use and redistribution of the characterization data, including as part of commercial and
non-commercial software, provided that the data are distributed unmodified and Fogra is identified as the source. The
designation FOGRAxx may be used solely to identify the respective reference data. Such use does not imply certification,
approval or endorsement by Fogra.

The library's generic CMYK profile is derived from this data by `tools/GenericCmyk`. It is this project's own profile, named
after this library; it is not a Fogra profile and Fogra has not certified, approved or endorsed it.

## Noto font subsets (test data only)

The files in `tests/testdata/fonts/` are subsets of Noto Sans (Copyright 2015 Google LLC) and Noto Sans CJK (Copyright 2014-2021
Adobe), made by `tools/TextFonts/make-test-fonts.py`: fewer glyphs, hinting removed, and in one face a synthetic legacy `kern`
table. The fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the license text is `tests/testdata/fonts/OFL.txt`. They are
used only by the test and benchmark projects and are not part of the shipped package.

`tests/testdata/fonts/NotoSans-Shaping-Subset.ttf` is a further Noto Sans subset (Copyright 2015 Google LLC, SIL Open Font License 1.1, same license text) made by `tools/TextFonts/make-shaping-fonts.py`: Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and the combining marks, hinting removed, with all of the font's GSUB, GPOS and GDEF layout kept. It is used only by the test and benchmark projects.
